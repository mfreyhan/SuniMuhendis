# Code of conduct

SuniMuhendis welcomes constructive participation from people with different
backgrounds and levels of experience.

Contributors must communicate respectfully, critique ideas and evidence rather
than people, disclose relevant conflicts, and avoid harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks, or publication of another person's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and
may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Report
conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[security advisory form](https://github.com/mfreyhan/SuniMuhendis/security/advisories/new).
Reports will be reviewed in confidence and addressed proportionately.
