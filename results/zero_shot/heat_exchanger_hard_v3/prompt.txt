Design a heat exchanger for the requirements below. Return ONLY one JSON object.

Seven fields are required:
{
  "geometry_type": "shell_and_tube or concentric_tube",
  "length": "tube length, metres, positive float",
  "inner_tube_di": "tube inside diameter, metres, positive float",
  "inner_tube_do": "tube outside diameter, metres, positive float",
  "outer_shell_di": "shell inside diameter, metres, positive float",
  "number_of_tubes": "positive integer",
  "baffle_spacing": "baffle spacing, metres, positive float"
}

These fields are optional. Omit any of them to accept its default, or set it to
take control of that decision. They are where a competent design differs from a
merely valid one:
{
  "tube_passes": "even integer, must divide number_of_tubes (default 2)",
  "pitch_ratio": "tube pitch / tube outside diameter, >= 1.25 (default 1.25)",
  "pitch_type": "triangular, square, 30deg, 45deg, 60deg or 90deg (default square)",
  "baffle_cut": "fraction of shell diameter, 0.15 to 0.45 (default 0.25)",
  "D_nozzle_hot": "hot-side nozzle bore, metres (default 0.05)",
  "D_nozzle_cold": "cold-side nozzle bore, metres (default 0.05)",
  "material": "carbon_steel, stainless_304, stainless_316, titanium or cupronickel (default carbon_steel)"
}

Do not output operating conditions or any field not listed above.

Fixed operating conditions (not design variables):
- Hot water, tube side: 2.5 kg/s, inlet 80 degrees C; rho=971.8 kg/m3, mu=0.000355 Pa.s, k=0.67 W/(m.K), Cp=4190 J/(kg.K), Pr=2.2.
- Cold water, shell side: 2.5 kg/s, inlet 20 degrees C; rho=998.2 kg/m3, mu=0.001002 Pa.s, k=0.598 W/(m.K), Cp=4182 J/(kg.K), Pr=7.0.
- Wall conductivity 50 W/(m.K); fouling resistance 1.76e-4 m2.K/W on each side.
- Maximum achievable duty is 627,300 W at 100% effectiveness; a one-shell-pass, two-tube-pass exchanger with these balanced flows asymptotes to 367,815 W.

Geometric rules (violating any of these is rejected before simulation):
- inner_tube_di < inner_tube_do < outer_shell_di, all positive.
- For shell_and_tube: at least 2 tubes, tube_passes even, number_of_tubes divisible by tube_passes, and baffle_spacing < length.
- The complete Phadkeb tube bundle, including pass lanes and shell-to-bundle clearance, must fit inside outer_shell_di. A lower-bound estimate is outer_shell_di > inner_tube_do + pitch_ratio*inner_tube_do*sqrt(4*number_of_tubes/pi); leave clearance above this.
- For concentric_tube: exactly 1 tube, tube_passes 1.
- Each nozzle bore must not exceed 35% of outer_shell_di.

Design limits. Each violation is one warning, and warnings are expensive:
- Tube-side velocity between 0.5 and 3.0 m/s.
- Shell-side crossflow velocity at most 2.0 m/s.
- Nozzle velocity between 1.0 and 5.0 m/s on both sides. A nozzle contributes 2 velocity heads of pressure loss, so bore is a real trade-off: too small and nozzle loss consumes the pressure budget, too large and the stream cannot distribute.
- Length / shell diameter between 3 and 15.
- Unsupported span (baffle_spacing) within the TEMA limit for the tube size: 1.118 m at 12.7 mm outside diameter, 1.524 m at 19.05 mm, 1.880 m at 25.4 mm, 2.540 m at 38.1 mm.
- LMTD correction factor F at least 0.75. F depends only on duty here, and falls below 0.75 above roughly 328,000 W, so overshooting the target is penalised.
- Minimum temperature approach at least 5 degrees C.
- Tube-side Reynolds number above 4,000; laminar or transitional tube flow is warned.
- Shell velocity below 80% of the fluid-elastic vibration critical velocity.

Objectives:
Deliver at least 250,000 W with BOTH tube-side and shell-side pressure losses at or below 10,000 Pa. Meeting those three requirements is the entry condition, not the goal. Past that point the score is earned by annualised cost and by freedom from warnings.

Exact Score V4 rules:
The three requirements are heat_duty_W >= 250000, dp_tube_Pa <= 10000, dp_shell_Pa <= 10000.
duty_progress = min(max(heat_duty_W / 250000, 0), 1).
For each pressure drop: progress = 1 if dp <= limit, otherwise max(1 - (dp - limit)/limit, 0).
gate_progress = mean of the three progress values.
If any requirement is unmet:  final_score = 0.30 * gate_progress.
If all three are met:
  cost_reward = 1 if annualised_cost <= 2000; 0 if annualised_cost >= 20000; otherwise (20000 - annualised_cost) / 18000.
  penalty_factor = max(1 - 0.10 * num_warnings, 0).
  final_score = 0.30 + 0.70 * cost_reward * penalty_factor.
Schema failure, geometric rejection, or simulation failure yields zero. Exceeding the duty target earns nothing extra, and costs score if it drives F below 0.75. Pressure losses below their limits all earn the same full credit, so the remaining score comes from building it cheaply and cleanly.
